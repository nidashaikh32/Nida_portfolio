01 / About

About Me

I'm a detail-oriented content professional with a strong interest in Islamic content research, verification and curation. I care about getting the details right: checking sources, cross-referencing them, and presenting knowledge in a way that is clear, respectful and easy to find.

My work so far has combined content management, independent research and writing, and teaching. That background has taught me to organise information carefully, explain ideas simply, and communicate well with the people I work with.

I'm also curious about technology. I enjoy learning how AI tools and digital platforms can support better research and better content, and I keep learning something new along the way.

Ek Lafz, Hazaar Maane

Project overview

Ek Lafz, Hazaar Maane is my independent Islamic content project. I research, write and publish each piece myself, with a focus on accuracy and on grounding content in reliable Islamic sources.

Reference areas

  • Hadith
  • Tafsir
  • Seerah

How it works

  1. 01

    Research

    Gather material on a topic from Hadith, Tafsir and Seerah.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Check the authenticity and context of what I find.

  3. 03

    Cross-reference

  4. 04

    Write & publish

    Turn the verified research into clear content and publish it independently.
